📝 Project Overview & Significance

This geospatial study presents a comparative assessment of post-monsoon inundation and persistent waterlogging in Barpeta district, Assam between 2024 and 2025. Utilizing high-resolution Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery captured uniformly on October 22, surface water was delineated using the Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI) alongside advanced SCL cloud-masking techniques. The quantitative analysis demonstrates a 20.1% reduction in waterlogged area—decreasing from 353.11 Sq. Km. (2024) to 281.98 Sq. Km. (2025). This study effectively captures prolonged water retention in low-lying topography and regional wetland ecosystems (Beels), providing essential baseline data for flood drainage management and agricultural mitigation planning.
